# Replaceable organization photographs

The starter images in src/lib/photos.json are genuine licensed Pexels stock photographs, NOT photos of Sunny Days Academy LLC.

To display approved REAL center photographs:

1. Place optimized photos here, for example hero.webp, about.webp, infants.webp.
2. Open src/lib/photos.json and replace a key's src with "/photos/hero.webp" (or the matching name).
3. Update alt to accurately describe the approved real photo. Keep the other keys unchanged.
4. Keep the photographer's authorization, license and any necessary parental/guardian consents on file.

Do not slightly edit an unlicensed photo and assume it becomes copyright-free.
