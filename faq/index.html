Ask a question
What ages does Sunny Days Academy accept?+

The Oklahoma child care listing includes infants (0–11 months), toddlers (12–23 months), preschool children (2–4 years) and school-age children (5 years and older).

What are the opening hours?+

The posted hours are Monday through Friday, 6:00 AM – 5:30 PM. The center is listed as closed on Saturdays and Sundays.

Is the center open year-round?+

Yes. Oklahoma Human Services lists Sunny Days Academy LLC as a year-round child care center.

Where is Sunny Days Academy located?+

You can find the center at 125 S Scraper, Vinita, OK 74301. Use the directions link on the Contact page.

How do I ask about openings or tuition?+

Call (918) 256-1416 or email sunnydaysacademy2026@gmail.com for current availability, tuition, and enrollment information.

Can I ask about arranging a visit?+

Yes. Contact the center to ask whether a visit can be arranged and what information to bring.

Who is listed as the director?+

Dana Brown is listed as the director in the Oklahoma Human Services provider record.
